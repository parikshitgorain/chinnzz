# Responsible Gambling

Gambling is entertainment, not income. Never gamble money you cannot afford to lose. If it stops being fun, stop.

## Before you play

- Set a budget before you start and treat it as spent.
- Set a time limit.
- Never chase losses.
- Never gamble to solve financial problems.
- Do not gamble while upset, intoxicated or under pressure.
- Take breaks away from the screen.

## Warning signs

- Spending more than you can afford.
- Borrowing money or selling things to gamble.
- Hiding gambling from family or friends.
- Feeling anxious, irritable or low about gambling.
- Chasing losses or raising stakes to feel the same excitement.
- Neglecting work, school or relationships.
- Gambling to escape stress or grief.

## If you need support

Support is free and confidential in many regions. Verify current numbers for your location before calling.

## Self-exclusion and tools

- Use deposit, loss and session limits offered by the operator.
- Use self-exclusion tools from the operator or regulator.
- Consider blocking software on your devices.
- Take a break from gambling content and communities if needed.
- Tell someone you trust if you are struggling.

## Age restriction

The site and the services it links to are for adults of legal gambling age. That age is 18+ or 21+ depending on the jurisdiction and product. If you are underage, leave the site.
